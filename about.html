About ApexGutter Co.

Dedicated exterior home protection, certified roofline safety standards, and precision water drainage craftsmanship.

Our History & Purpose

Safeguarding Properties from the Destructive Force of Stormwater

ApexGutter was founded on a straightforward principle: residential rooflines deserve professional, meticulous water management rather than careless shortcuts.

Most homeowners only think about their gutters when water begins pouring over the front door or pooling in the basement. Our mission is to take the danger and guesswork out of gutter maintenance through proactive inspections, zero-mess hand cleaning, and heavy-gauge continuous seamless installations that last decades.

Integrity in Every Quote

We only recommend the repairs your property genuinely needs, providing transparent photo evidence for every recommendation.

Zero-Mess Lawn Promise

Every leaf, twig, and handful of shingle grit is bagged and removed. We leave your landscaping spotless.

Safety First Protocol

Our Safety & Workmanship Standards

Gutter maintenance is among the most hazardous home maintenance tasks. Here is how our certified technicians eliminate risks:

OSHA Compliance

Rigorous fall-arrest harness tie-offs and anchor certifications for two-story and steep-pitch rooflines.

Ladder Standoffs

Specialized standoff stabilizers that rest safely on roof decking rather than denting your aluminum gutter troughs.

$2M Liability Coverage

Comprehensive commercial general liability and workers' compensation coverage protecting both crew and property.

Digital Inspections

High-resolution diagnostic photo documentation delivered to your inbox for complete transparency.
