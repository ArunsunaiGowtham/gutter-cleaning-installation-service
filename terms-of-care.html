Terms of Care

Customer Service Agreement & Workmanship Standards • ApexGutter Co.

1. Scope of Care & Services

ApexGutter provides professional hand removal of gutter debris, high-flow hydro downspout snaking, gutter realignment, and on-site continuous seamless aluminum fabrication. Work is completed according to agreed estimates.

2. Property Access & Preparation

Homeowners are asked to ensure clear ground-level ladder placement around the roof perimeter and provide access to a standard exterior water hose spigot for downspout flushing.

3. Safety & Weather Policy

Technician safety is governed by strict OSHA standards. Services will be rescheduled without penalty in the event of active thunderstorms, lightning, freezing ice, or extreme high winds.

4. Warranties & Guarantees
  • 30-Day Cleanflow Guarantee: If gutters or downspouts clog within 30 days of a full cleanout, we return and clear them at no charge.
  • 10-Year Craftsmanship Warranty: All continuous seamless aluminum installations include a 10-year pitch, hanger, and joint seal warranty.
5. Rescheduling & Cancellations

We request 24 hours of advance notice for non-emergency cancellations or date changes. There are no fees for weather-related postponements initiated by our dispatch team.

6. Questions & Support

For questions regarding service care, warranty claims, or work order adjustments:
