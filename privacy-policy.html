Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • ApexGutter Co.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect essential details required to prepare estimates and execute gutter maintenance: your full name, property service address, contact phone number, and email. We do not collect unnecessary tracking data.

2. How We Use Information

Your information is utilized solely for:

  • Generating accurate roofline cleaning and seamless extrusion quotes.
  • Dispatching technicians and sending on-the-way SMS notices.
  • Issuing receipts and maintaining your 30-day cleanflow warranty records.
3. No Selling of Personal Data

ApexGutter never sells, rents, or shares your personal details with third-party marketers or advertisers under any circumstances.

4. Data Security

All customer records and estimate submissions are processed through encrypted SSL connections and stored on secured infrastructure to prevent unauthorized access.

5. Your Rights & Contact

You may request review or deletion of your service records at any time by contacting our customer care department:
